# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute without being harassed,
insulted or dismissed — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Review the change, not the author.
- Assume good faith, ask before assuming, and accept that you may be wrong.
- Keep discussion in English and on topic.
- Respect that maintainers decide scope; a rejected proposal is not a personal attack.
- Give credit for work you build on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, slurs or sexualised comments or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.
- Dismissing a report of a problem with "just don't use it".

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code review and any space where
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to `gothwadtech@gmail.com`. Include what happened, where and
who was involved. Maintainers will review it and respond as quickly as they can, and will
keep the reporter's identity confidential.

Consequences escalate with severity: a private warning, a temporary ban from the
repository, or a permanent ban. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions
that break these rules.
